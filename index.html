MTRS / SDI-BCA Toolkit

A reference implementation of the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test and Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis — two open-source methods for evaluating transport policy scenarios under uncertainty and stage dependency.

01 / Overview

Two methods, one question: how much should we trust a scenario ranking?

Transport policy appraisal produces ranked scenario recommendations. MTRS measures how fragile that ranking is to six sources of uncertainty; SDI-BCA measures how programme delays propagate through stage dependencies and change the economic case.

Method 1

MTRS — Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability

Takes a decision matrix of policy scenarios scored on transport indicators and an uncertainty specification, then decomposes ranking instability into six sources — model structure, parameters, data year, policy mapping, indicator weights, and metropolitan-area structure — and evaluates three reporting rules.

Introduced in Research Report No. 212 (May 2024).

Read the MTRS documentation →
Method 2

SDI-BCA — Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis

Models a staged transport programme as a dependency graph: a stage opens only when its own construction is complete and every prerequisite stage is operational. Delays propagate downstream, and overlapping delays interact — captured as a staged-delay interaction term.

Introduced in Research Report No. 231 (June 2025).

Read the SDI-BCA documentation →
02 / Reporting framework

Three reporting rules

Question MTRS rule Default threshold
May the ranking be reported as stable? Stability score S ≥ threshold, and no reversal risk S ≥ 0.80
May the ranking reverse? Top-ranked scenario flip probability over the ensemble p > 0.10
Does the ranking transfer to another metropolitan-area structure? Share of alternative-area draws reproducing the base ranking failure ≤ 0.30
All thresholds are configurable. The headline verdict is STABLE, REVERSAL or NON-TRANSFERABLE.
03 / Installation

Five minutes

terminal 
$ git clone https://github.com/fanshiyu35/mtrs-sdibca-toolkit.git
$ cd mtrs-sdibca-toolkit
$ pip install .            # optional; runs from the source tree too
$ python3 examples/mtrs_example.py
$ python3 examples/sdibca_example.py
